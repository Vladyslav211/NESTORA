Customer care

Returns & refunds

We want you to be happy with your purchase. Here's how returns and refunds work.

30-day returns

Eligible products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, undamaged and returned in their original condition and packaging.

How to start a return

Contact our customer care team with your order number and the reason for your return. We will provide instructions for the next steps.

Return shipping

Unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect, return shipping costs may be the responsibility of the customer.

Refunds

Once your return has been received and inspected, we will process the refund to the original payment method. Processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or contains the wrong item, contact us as soon as possible. Include your order number and clear photos of the issue so we can help resolve it.

Non-returnable items

Certain products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene, customization or other product-specific restrictions. Any applicable exclusions will be stated on the product page.
