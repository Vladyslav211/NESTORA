Shipping & delivery

Everything you need to know about getting your NESTORA. order delivered.

Order processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days. During promotional periods or holidays, processing may take slightly longer.

Delivery times

Estimated delivery times depend on your destination and the shipping method available at checkout.

Destination Estimated delivery
United States 5–10 business days
Canada 7–14 business days

Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email containing your tracking information.

Delayed orders

If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, please contact our customer care team with your order number.

International orders

Depending on the destination, additional customs duties or taxes may apply. Any applicable charges are the responsibility of the customer.
